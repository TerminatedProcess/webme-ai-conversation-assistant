Home NAS: decision summary

Family photo + backup storage, ~4 TB today, growing ~1 TB/year · Last updated 2026-11-26 14:05

⚠ Don't get this wrong: RAID is not a backup. Whatever you buy, keep one off-site copy (cloud or a drive at a relative's).

Current recommendation

  • 2-bay box, 2 × 8 TB mirrored: 8 TB usable covers ~4 years of growth.
  • Off-site: cloud backup of the photos folder only: about 1.5 TB, cheapest tier is enough.
  • Skip the 4-bay: extra cost isn't justified until you pass 8 TB.
